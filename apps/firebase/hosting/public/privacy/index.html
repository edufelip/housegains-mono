Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-13

Who we are

HouseGains is developed by Eduardo Felipe, an individual developer. He is the controller of your personal data. Contact: support@housegains.app. Website: https://housegains.app.

Data we collect

  • Account data: display name, email address, and the sign-in identifier from Apple or Google.
  • Household content: household name, icon and color, chores, subtasks, assignments, completions, XP and feed entries.
  • Device push token: only if you turn on reminders.
  • Preferences: language and visual style.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: collected by Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix bugs.

We do not collect location, contacts or photos. We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps and websites, and we do not sell data.

How we use it

  • To run your account and households. Legal bases: performance of a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); LGPD art. 7, V).
  • To send reminders you asked for. Legal basis: consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a); LGPD art. 7, I). You can turn them off at any time.
  • To find and fix crashes and keep the app stable. Legal basis: legitimate interests (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); LGPD art. 7, IX).
  • To keep the service secure and answer your requests. Legal basis: legitimate interests and legal obligations.

Who else sees your data

Other members of your household see your display name, your completions and your XP.

We use Google Firebase as our processor: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Hosting. Its servers are in the United States, so your data is transferred there. We do not share personal data in any other way.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Settings, then Delete account) or by emailing support@housegains.app. We delete the data within 30 days. Past completions stay in the household history under "Former member". You can leave a household at any time in Settings.

Children

HouseGains is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EU and EEA. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can access, correct, delete and port your data, and object to some processing. This applies under the GDPR (EU and EEA), the LGPD (Brazil) and the CCPA (California). You can complain to your national supervisory authority (GDPR) or to the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority, ANPD (LGPD).

California: we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To use your rights, email support@housegains.app. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and Google encrypts it at rest. Access to the Firebase project is limited to the developer.

Changes

We may update this page and its date. For material changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect, where the law requires it.

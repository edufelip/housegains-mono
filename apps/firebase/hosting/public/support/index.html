Frequently asked questions

How do I join a household?

Ask a housemate for their invite code, link or QR code. In HouseGains, choose Join and enter the 6-character code. You can also open the invite link on your phone.

My invite code does not work.

Codes expire after 7 days and stop working after 8 uses. Ask the household creator for a new code.

How do I leave a household?

Open Settings and tap Leave household. Your past completions and XP stay in the household history, shown as "Former member".

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and tap Delete account, or follow the steps on the delete account page. Your data is deleted within 30 days.

How do reminders work?

Reminders are optional. Turn them on in the app and allow notifications on your phone. We store a push token only while reminders are on. You can turn them off at any time.

Who can see what I do?

Other members of your household see your display name, your completions and your XP.

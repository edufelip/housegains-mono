Delete your HouseGains account

You can delete your account in the app or ask us by email. Both lead to the same result.

Delete in the app

  1. Open HouseGains and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted and your data is removed within 30 days.

Delete by email

Send an email from the address linked to your account to support@housegains.app with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm that you control the account. We then delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your display name, email address and sign-in link to Apple or Google.
  • Your push token and reminder setting.
  • Your language and visual style preferences.

What is kept

Your past completions, XP and feed entries stay in your household history, shown as "Former member". Other members keep their history.

Only want to leave a household?

Open Settings and tap Leave household. Your account stays active.

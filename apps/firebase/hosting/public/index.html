HouseGains

Housework, but make it a team game.

HouseGains helps people who share a home split chores, see who did what, and earn XP for every subtask they finish. Roommates, couples and families, one shared board.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. 1

    Make or join a household

    Create one and invite your housemates with a 6-character code, a link or a QR code.

  2. 2

    Split chores into subtasks

    Pick a chore, break it into steps and assign it to someone, or leave it open for anyone.

  3. 3

    Finish, earn, climb

    Each subtask earns XP for the person who did it. Finish the whole chore and everyone who helped shares a bonus. Check the weekly leaderboard and keep your streak going.

Three looks, one household

Pick the look you like in the app. It changes only how the app looks, never your chores or XP.

  • Arcade

    Dark quest cards, neon green progress and an RPG feel.

  • Sticker Pop

    Butter yellow, bold black outlines and hard shadows.

  • Soft Home

    Calm slate tones and progress rings that are easy on the eyes.

The basics

  • Free. No ads. No in-app purchases.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google or email.
  • Optional reminders before a chore is due.
  • Six languages: English, Português, Español, Français, Deutsch and Italiano.

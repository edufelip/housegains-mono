# BR-413 HouseGains Domain Rules

**Status:** In progress. These are the active product rules for HouseGains v1; production enforcement is not verified.

1. **Household identity and profile:** Each household has one creator and members. Chore assignees and completion contributors must be members of that household. The creator may update household name, icon, color, and photo in Settings; a profile edit preserves household identity, captured timezone, membership, and invite state.
2. **Catalog copy:** Adding a catalog chore creates an independent household-owned copy. Catalog source changes never mutate the copy.
3. **Assignments:** A chore may have zero, one, or several assignees. Empty assignment means Unassigned, not invalid. Assignment does not restrict who may help.
4. **Builder limits:** Each chore contains 1–10 ordered subtasks. Each subtask awards 5–100 XP. Completion bonus is 0–50 XP.
5. **Recurrence:** Supported forms are daily, weekly on selected days, and every N days (N=1–365), with a due time. Household creation captures the device's IANA timezone; recurrence calendar days and due times use that stored zone. Spring-forward gaps shift by the gap duration; repeated fall-back wall times use the earlier matching instant.
6. **Definition ownership:** Only the chore creator may edit, pause, or delete its definition and future instances.
7. **Snapshot immutability:** Existing instances retain the definition, subtask, bonus, assignment, and due-time values captured for that occurrence. Later definition edits affect future instances only.
8. **Invite validity:** A code has exactly six characters from ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789, is valid for seven days, and allows at most eight successful joins. Regeneration revokes the prior code. Code, QR, and deep link resolve to the same invite.
9. **Unique completion:** One subtask has at most one credited completion per instance. A successful completion stores the instance, subtask, member, XP, completion instant, and assist attribution.
10. **Subtask XP:** XP is credited to the member recorded as completing the subtask. The same completion cannot be awarded twice.
11. **Member help:** Any household member may complete a subtask even if the chore is assigned to somebody else or is unassigned. Assignment is display/responsibility metadata, not an authorization gate for helping.
12. **Completion bonus:** Award the bonus only if every subtask is complete before the instance due time. Split integer XP evenly among distinct contributing members; give indivisible remainder points to the member completing the final subtask.
13. **Partial closure:** A partially closed instance keeps its earned subtask ledger entries and receives no completion bonus.
14. **Undo window:** A member may undo only their own completion, within five minutes of its completion instant. Undo removes that subtask's XP effect and, if the instance had paid a bonus, reverses the instance bonus awards and reopens the instance as appropriate.
15. **Local/demo boundary:** Local fixture behavior must be labeled as local/demo. It is not evidence of a remote invite, cross-device membership, synchronized feed, or production ledger.
16. **Local XP scorecard:** Weekly totals sum signed ledger events from Monday 00:00 in the household timezone. This slice's streak is consecutive household-timezone dates with net-positive XP for the current member; if today has no positive total yet, calculation may continue from yesterday. Level and title follow rule 25.
17. **Admin rights:** Admin rights mean role `creator` or `admin`. The creator grants and removes the admin role (creator-only, confirmed, never on themselves). Existing creator-only operations stay creator-only; admin rights add only the moderation powers.
18. **Dispute window:** A completion can be disputed or moderated within 72 hours of its completion time. A resolved or expired dispute can no longer be reported; the same member cannot report the same instance twice.
19. **Verdicts:** `done` keeps every disputed completion; `not_done` revokes all of them; `partial` revokes a non-empty strict subset chosen by the reviewer and is unavailable when fewer than two steps are disputed. Revocation reverses the step XP (and the instance bonus when the instance was done) through `revoke-*` ledger rows, records who acted and the verdict on the instance, and reopens the instance per the undo status rule.
20. **Conflict of interest:** An admin who completed one of the disputed or moderated steps cannot pass the verdict unless they are the only member with admin rights; the backend rejects the attempt and the client shows the reason. A dispute verdict is checked against every disputed step; a direct moderation is checked against the steps it targets only, so a conflicted admin cannot mark the whole chore not done but may still give a partial verdict on the other members' steps, which are the only ones the picker offers. A direct moderation also resolves the instance's open dispute, so the check spans the targets plus the completions that open dispute still covers (its `completionIds` that match the current completions; a legacy dispute without `completionIds` covers its `subtaskIds`): when one of those is the admin's own, neither "Mark as not done" nor the partial picker is offered and the conflict note shows instead. The Reports screen checks the same covered set, not every disputed `subtaskId`.
21. **Weekly standings snapshot:** Ranks shows only the latest closed week written by the scheduled job (top member and wall of shame, both possibly null). Moderation after a close never rewrites the snapshot; the client renders it read-only.

22. **Program gate:** Gamification UI requires `gamification_enabled === true` (Remote Config, bundled default `false`, read once per cold start like the style picker config) AND `households/{hid}.gamificationCohort === 'live'`. Anything else (flag off, `control`, `shadow`, missing or unknown cohort) leaves the app unchanged and starts no gamification listener. The server also returns `celebrations: []` and writes no inbox rows outside live mode and a live cohort.
23. **Celebration delivery:** Celebrations come from the `celebrations` array of `completeSubtask` / `postCompletion` (the caller's own) and from `users/{uid}/inbox` (async earns). They are deduped by celebration id. Inbox rows are acknowledged with `ackCelebrations` (1–50 ids) only after they were shown; rows older than three days are acknowledged without being shown. If an acknowledgement fails, the ids stay queued and the call is retried with a bounded backoff (2 s doubling to 30 s, five retries; a manual retry restarts it) and also goes out with the next batch, so a celebration can at worst show once more in a later session. Retrying a listener keeps what was already shown or queued, so an unacknowledged inbox row is never celebrated twice in a session. The inbox listener has its own status: its failure only delays celebrations and never puts the wallet screen in error. A `level` celebration carries the new-curve level only; when it is at or below the member's level floor (`levelFloor`, or the old-curve level before the backfill) it is acknowledged without being shown, and a shown one displays the level the rest of the app displays. That check applies to the `ganhos` track only: an organizer (`track: 'zelo'`, levels 1–5 for Zelo 0/200/600/1500/3000, id `cel:level:organizer:{level}:{uid}`) level-up is always shown, framed as the house organizer's with the organizer title of that level (Organizer, House Manager, Seasoned Manager, Chief Manager, Legendary Manager), and is never compared with the member level.
24. **One full screen per session:** At most one full-screen achievement unlock (`badge` with `fullScreen: true`, silver and gold) per app session. Later full-screen badges and every bronze badge (`fullScreen: false`) are toasts. When the action also completed the instance (and the S3 screen opens), the badge earned by that action is shown inside S3 and does not use the session's full-screen slot.
25. **Levels:** `xpForLevel(L) = 50·L·(L−1)`; displayed level `max(levelFloor ?? floor(xp/100)+1, levelForXp(xpPeak ?? xp))`; titles Rookie 1–2, Helper 3–4, Tidy Titan 5–7, Clean Master 8–11, Home Legend 12–15, House Guardian 16+. `xpPeak` never decreases, so an undo or a revoke never lowers the displayed level. Progress to the next level is measured on `xpPeak`, never negative.
26. **Privacy:** The wallet (`wallets/{uid}` and its ledger) is readable only by its owner and is shown only to the owner. The organizer projection (`households/{hid}/organizer/{creatorUid}`) is readable by every member and carries Care (Zelo), never a spendable balance. Redemption is out of scope: no spend path exists in the app.
27. **Away mode:** `setAwayWeeks` replaces the future away weeks with Mondays strictly after today in the household time zone, at most 3 per calendar year counting past weeks (`invalid-argument` for a non-Monday, `failed-precondition` for a past week or the yearly limit). The app validates the same rules before the call and shows server errors as localized text; an away week keeps the streak frozen and reminders quiet.
28. **Streak states:** A streak with `current > 0` shows the counter and the record. With `current = 0` and a record it shows only the calm message "Sua sequência descansou. Recorde: N semanas." (no zero, no red, no danger color). With no record it invites the first counted week (3 net subtask credits).
29. **Rate-the-app banner:** Today shows a banner asking a signed-in user to rate HouseGains in the store. It never offers points, Ganhos, XP or any other reward, and no copy implies one (store policies forbid incentivised ratings). State is kept per user uid on the device (AsyncStorage key `housegains.rating.prompt.v1.{uid}`, `{ firstSeenAt, dismissedAt, ratedAt }`); clients cannot write arbitrary `users/{uid}` fields, so nothing is stored in Firestore. `firstSeenAt` is recorded the first time the user is seen on the install and the banner is hidden until 14 days later. "Not now" sets `dismissedAt` and hides the banner for another 14 days. "Rate HouseGains" asks the OS for the native review dialog (`expo-store-review`) when it is available, otherwise opens the store page; stores never say whether a rating was submitted, so `ratedAt` means "the user tapped Rate" and the banner then never shows again. When neither the dialog nor a store URL is reachable (or the OS call fails) the tap is logged and handled like "Not now". Timestamps in the future (the clock went backwards) are pulled back to now, corrupt or missing stored state restarts the 14 days, and any storage failure is logged and leaves the banner hidden for that session. The banner is independent of the gamification gate (rule 22).

## Open rule details

- Define how production handles later household timezone changes. The local adapter captures the creation zone and shifts nonexistent daylight-saving wall times forward by the gap duration; repeated times use the earlier matching instant.
- Define the rank period and tie policy.
- Define the exact predicate that sets assist attribution; the active contract requires that it be stored but does not specify its derivation.
- Define server transaction and idempotency boundaries before claiming collaborative production XP.


Implementation review clarifications: recurrence equivalence compares parsed rules without changing the stored anchor; editable future deadlines are recalculated when due time changes. Earned instance metadata (name, icon, recurrence and due time) remains snapshotted and displayable after a definition is renamed or deleted. Catalog copies use source IDs rather than matching user-visible names. Mutation failures retain assignment drafts and expose retry; theme persistence failures also expose an explicit retry.

New households start with a recorded 400 XP weekly goal. The scorecard bars and Soft Home ring use weekly earned XP divided by that goal, clamped visually to 100%; daily set progress is reported separately. A missing or non-positive target renders an explicit goal-not-set state without a fabricated percentage. Goal editing is not part of this slice.

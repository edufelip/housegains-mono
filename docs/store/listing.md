## en-US

### App Store

#### name
HouseGains: Chores & XP

#### subtitle
Split chores, earn XP

#### promotional text
Turn housework into a team game. Split chores into subtasks, earn XP for each one, and see who is carrying their share on the weekly board.

#### description
HouseGains turns housework into a team game for people who share a home.

Create a household, invite your housemates with a 6-character code, a link or a QR code, and start splitting the chores.

HOW IT WORKS
- Pick a chore and break it into subtasks.
- Assign it to someone, or leave it open for anyone to help with.
- Finish a subtask and earn XP for it. Finish the whole chore and the people who helped share a completion bonus.
- Follow the weekly leaderboard and keep your streak going.

FOR ROOMMATES, COUPLES AND FAMILIES
Everyone sees who did what. Your display name, your completions and your XP are visible to the people in your household, so credit is clear and nobody has to keep score in their head.

THREE LOOKS
Arcade: dark quest cards and neon green progress. Sticker Pop: butter yellow, bold outlines and hard shadows. Soft Home: calm slate tones and progress rings. The look you pick changes only the appearance.

SIMPLE, FAIR AND FREE
- Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
- Sign in with Apple, Google or email.
- Optional reminders when your chore is due.
- Six languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian.

HouseGains asks for no location, contacts or photos. Read our privacy policy at https://housegains.app/privacy/

#### keywords
household,roommates,couples,family,split,tasks,cleaning,shared,streak,ranking,reminders,gamified

## Review and compliance (English)

### App Review notes
Demo account (fill in before submission; keep it working for review):
- Email: [DEMO EMAIL]
- Password: [DEMO PASSWORD]

How to test:
1. Sign in with the demo account (email option). Sign in with Apple and Google are also offered on the sign-in screen.
2. If the demo account has no household, create one from the first screens. Pick a name, an icon and a color.
3. Join flow: from the household's invite screen, copy the 6-character code (or the link, or show the QR code). On a second device or a second account, choose Join, enter the code, and confirm. The second account then appears in the household.
4. Completing subtasks: open Today, open a chore, and tap a subtask to complete it. XP is credited to the person who completed it. Complete every subtask to see the completion bonus split among the people who helped.
5. Undo: the person who completed a subtask can undo it within five minutes.
6. Account deletion: Settings, then Delete account. Use a throwaway account for this step.

Notes for review:
- The app is free. It has no in-app purchases, no ads and no third-party tracking.
- Push notifications are optional. The app asks for permission only after reminders are turned on.
- Households are private. Only members of a household see its data.
- Support: https://housegains.app/support/
